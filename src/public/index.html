Brand New Sealed Phone Setup (Day-0)

For unboxed or factory-reset devices before customer delivery:

  1. Power on the factory-reset device to the Welcome screen.
  2. Tap the blank space 6 times to launch the built-in enterprise QR scanner.
  3. Scan this QR code. The device downloads app-release.apk and self-provisions.
  4. Tap "FINISH & CONTINUE" to enter the home screen with full hardware guard active.
