Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026

1. Regulatory Compliance Framework

This Privacy Policy describes how Prithon Tech Private Limited collects, processes, and stores data through its Mobile Device Management (MDM) client application and centralized management infrastructure. Our policies adhere strictly to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Regulatory Framework for Digital Lending.

2. Scope of Collected Technical Data

Our client software operates under the Android Enterprise Device Owner framework. We collect strictly limited device-level telemetry essential to verify asset security, loan servicing status, and hardware integrity. We collect:

  • Cryptographic hashes of hardware identifiers (SHA-256 tokenized IMEI and serial numbers).
  • Device manufacturer, model name, and operating system build version.
  • System battery capacity, charging state, and active network connection type (Wi-Fi or Cellular).
  • Time stamps of synchronization heartbeats and policy execution acknowledgments.
Critical Privacy Guarantee:

Our software does not inspect, read, copy, upload, or process any personal user content. This includes personal photographs, gallery media, video files, personal contact lists, SMS text messages, third-party chat records (e.g., WhatsApp), call recordings, and personal web browsing history. We do not access camera or microphone streams outside explicit system barcode scanning workflows.

3. Emergency Access Protection

In full compliance with telecommunication regulations and statutory public safety requirements, our remote device management and kiosk restrictions never block, restrict, or interfere with public emergency calling. Emergency access to numbers including 112, 100, and 108 remains functional regardless of device payment or lock status.

4. Data Storage and Cryptographic Security

All data in transit is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3). Static identifiers and device secrets are stored utilizing hardware-backed Android Keystore modules on the client and AES-256 encrypted database volumes at rest. Production access is restricted to authorized operations personnel through multi-factor authentication and logged in immutable audit records.

5. Data Retention and Account De-Registration

Device records remain active solely during the contractual duration of the relevant loan or financing agreement. Upon full settlement of all obligations and generation of a formal No Objection Certificate (NOC), the central system issues a permanent de-provisioning command that removes all Device Owner privileges and initiates agent removal.
